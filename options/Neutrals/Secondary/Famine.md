Once the Baker has a set amount of people with bread alive, they will become Famine. If the Famine does not get voted out after the meeting, then they will become Famine, and every player without bread will starve (excluding other Apocalypse members). After this starvation of everyone without bread, Famine can use their Kill Button to starve any remaining players, which will kill those players right before the next meeting.<br>
